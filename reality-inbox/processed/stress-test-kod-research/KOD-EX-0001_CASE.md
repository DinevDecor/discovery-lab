# EX-0001 — Origins of KOD

## Mission

Reconstruct the origin and early evolution of KOD.

The objective is not to preserve conclusions.

The objective is to preserve intellectual evolution.

---

## Primary Question

When did KOD become a distinct research methodology rather than a collection of ideas?

---

## Scope

This excavation covers the earliest conversations that contributed to the emergence of KOD.

---

## Success Criteria

The excavation should identify:

- the first recurring research questions;
- the first core ideas;
- the first hypotheses;
- the first candidate principles;
- the first architectural concepts;
- the first methodological changes.

---

## Deliverables

- Excavation Report
- Timeline
- Knowledge Objects
- Research Sessions
- Integration into Corpus
