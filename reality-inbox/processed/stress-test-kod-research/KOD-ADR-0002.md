# ADR-0002

## Title

KOD protects honest research, not ideas.

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

KOD was initially developed as a system for organizing knowledge, principles, reasoning and decision improvement.

Further research showed that this is not sufficient.

A system that only stores or develops knowledge can still become biased if it starts protecting its own conclusions.

KOD must therefore protect the integrity of the research process itself.

---

## Decision

KOD does not protect ideas.

KOD protects honest research.

AI systems working within KOD must not act as final authorities.

Their role is to protect the method by challenging assumptions, tracking uncertainty, preserving rejected hypotheses and requiring contact with reality.

Reality remains the final arbiter.

---

## Consequences

KOD must include or support:

- an executable research constitution;
- a Research Guardian;
- a Research Journal;
- traceability from conclusion back to observation and evidence;
- clear separation between observation, hypothesis, candidate principle, principle and axiom;
- preservation of rejected ideas and failed falsification attempts.

This decision changes KOD from a knowledge system into a self-correcting research operating system.
