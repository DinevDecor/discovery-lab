# ADR-0001

## Title

KOD is a methodology before it is software.

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

KOD will generate multiple software systems such as Trust Engine, Regime AI, Dinev Decor AI and future intelligent agents.

Without a clear architectural decision, KOD could gradually become just another software project.

---

## Decision

KOD is the methodology.

Software systems are applications built on top of KOD.

No product defines KOD.

KOD defines the products.

---

## Consequences

The methodology remains independent from:

- programming languages
- AI models
- software frameworks
- future technologies

Products may evolve independently while sharing the same methodology.
