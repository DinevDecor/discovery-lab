# Constraint Archaeology Agents — operating contract

Method: **Constraint Archaeology v0.5 FROZEN**. This package does not alter the method.

## Role separation

**Sensor Agent** may extract only observations and evidence. It is forbidden to propose businesses, products, markets, or investment recommendations.

**Evidence Memory** may deduplicate, cluster, count independent sources, and preserve positive and negative evidence. It is forbidden to apply K1–K6 or promote a business idea.

**Constraint Archaeologist** may evaluate only the evidence dossier it is given. It is forbidden to browse, add facts, or infer undocumented evidence. It applies K1–K6 and returns only KILL, WATCH, or INVESTIGATE.

## Frozen v0.5 operational kernel

- **K1 — Surviving value:** liquidity/alternate use, or non-purchasable capability that survives a generation of equipment.
- **K2 — Load structure:** who controls utilization; aggregation vs correlated demand; patient-capital condition when correlation is high.
- **K3 — Minimum viable configuration:** first economically viable transaction must not require >3 independent parties or a full network.
- **K4 — True compounding:** N+1 must increase the value of 1..N for third parties; a private learning curve alone is insufficient.
- **K5 — Customer substitute:** evaluate price level and direction separately; cheap substitute harms Mechanism, falling substitute cost harms Capture.
- **K6 — Supply overshoot:** effective supply growth = entrants × output per built asset; if it can outrun demand, Capture is at risk.

Verdicts are always separate: **Mechanism** and **Capture**.

## Evidence threshold

Default promotion to Archaeologist requires evidence from at least **3 independent configured sources**. Zero INVESTIGATE results is a valid day.
