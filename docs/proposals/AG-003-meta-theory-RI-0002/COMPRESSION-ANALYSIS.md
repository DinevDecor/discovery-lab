# Compression Analysis — RI-0002

Per `META-THEORY-REPORT.md` Q7. Optimized for explanatory power, not
elegance, per the task's own instruction — each step below states
exactly what coverage is kept and what precision is lost, rather than
presenting the smaller set as simply "better."

**Fixed baseline, never covered at any compression level**: `RI-4`
(the 3-level Constitution/Protocol/Prompt structure — organizational,
not a testable claim), `RI-13` (Trust Engine — a different named
project), `RI-18` (an economic hypothesis and a product concept, not a
methodology claim), `RT-2` (AG-002 itself already declined this as a
real connection). These four are excluded honestly at every stage below,
not folded in at the end to make a total look cleaner.

## 10 principles

1. Reality is the final arbiter of any claim, not internal coherence or
   authorship. *(`RI-3`)*
2. A submitted claim is judged by an independent, non-authorial
   mechanism that classifies it and applies a type-specific test.
   *(`RI-1`, `RI-2`)*
3. No perspective — human or AI — has unmediated access to reality; this
   is why external validation is necessary, not merely careful. *(`RI-16`)*
4. Generation and validation are architecturally separate phases;
   neither substitutes for the other. *(`RI-15`)*
5. A minimal hypothesis survives only by being attacked with independent
   models; what fails is discarded, and the smallest surviving core is
   kept. *(`RI-17`, `RT-4`, drawing on `RI-10`'s Evidence Ladder/Breaker
   Mode)*
6. Discovery proceeds by stripping system-specific detail across
   radically different domains until an invariant process remains.
   *(`RI-5`, `RT-3`, `RI-6`)*
7. A candidate found this way is not yet accepted — it remains a
   candidate pending independent testing. *(`RI-7`)*
8. Change to an established architecture is gated: a new idea becomes an
   Investigation, validated against the existing corpus, before it is
   applied. *(`RI-9`)*
9. Authoritative state lives in one store, which changes only through
   proposals, never automatically. *(`RI-8`, `RI-11`, `RI-12`)*
10. What is already established should not be defended for its own
    sake, and no whole should be assumed to permanently control or
    outlast its parts. *(`RT-1`, `RI-14`)*

**Coverage: 14 of 18 Recovered Ideas, 3 of 4 Repeated Themes.** No
precision lost yet — this level is close enough to the raw findings that
each principle maps to a small, specific citation set.

## 5 principles

1. **Validation discipline** — claims earn standing only through
   independent, non-authorial testing. *(merges 10-principle #1–4)*
2. **Survival testing** — minimal hypotheses are kept only if they
   survive adversarial attack; the smallest surviving core is retained.
   *(merges #5)*
3. **Generative abstraction** — strip system-specific detail across
   domains to find invariant processes. *(merges #6–7)*
4. **Gated, centrally-proposed growth** — architectural and knowledge
   change happens only through gated proposals against one authoritative
   store. *(merges #8–9)*
5. **Non-attachment to current state** — don't defend conclusions for
   their own sake; no whole outlasts or controls its parts by default.
   *(merges #10)*

**Coverage: unchanged, 14 of 18 / 3 of 4** — but **precision is
already lost here**: merging `RI-9` (a governance rule about how ideas
become architecture) with `RI-8`/`RI-11`/`RI-12` (Registry-specifics)
under one "gated growth" principle risks implying they are restatements
of each other. `KMP-0001` (the existing, on-record curation decision
over `RI-8`/`RI-12`) already found real distinctions between them — this
compression does not undo that finding, but a reader encountering only
the 5-principle level, without `KMP-0001`, could mistake consolidation
for identity. Flagged here so it is not mistaken later.

## 3 principles

1. **External validation is the only source of standing for a claim.**
   *(merges 5-principle #1–2: validation discipline + survival testing)*
2. **Discovery is cross-domain abstraction.** *(= 5-principle #3,
   unchanged)*
3. **Growth is gated and non-attached to current state.** *(merges
   5-principle #4–5)*

**Coverage: unchanged, 14 of 18 / 3 of 4** — **further precision lost**:
merging "validation discipline" with "survival testing" blurs `RI-15`'s
specific architectural claim (generation and validation must be
*structurally separate*) into a broader, vaguer "external validation
matters" statement. `RI-15`'s own distinguishing content — the
separation itself, tested for convergence across five domains — is the
single most conceptually central finding in the whole graph
(`EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md`), and this compression is the first level where
its specific claim stops being recoverable from the principle's wording
alone.

## 1 candidate foundational principle

> **Nothing earns standing by being asserted; it earns standing only by
> surviving an independent, adversarial check against reality — and
> what survives should be kept as small as possible.**

**Coverage, stated honestly, not rounded up**: this principle cleanly
covers `RI-1`, `RI-2`, `RI-3`, `RI-9`, `RI-10`, `RI-15`, `RI-16`,
`RI-17`, `RT-1`, `RT-4` — 10 items, the validation-discipline core.

**It does not cleanly cover the generative-abstraction cluster —
`RI-5`, `RT-3` (the single most-repeated idea in the entire archive,
five independent restatements), `RI-6`, `RI-7`.** Discovery-by-
abstraction is a claim about *how candidates are generated*, logically
prior to and distinct from *how they are tested* — collapsing it into
"survives an adversarial check" is a real stretch, not a natural fit.
**This is the most important honest weakness of the one-principle
compression**: it fails to cleanly explain the archive's own strongest
single evidentiary signal. A beautiful one-sentence theory that cannot
account for the most-repeated idea in its own source material is a
failure by this report's own governing rule, not a success dressed down
to look modest.

`RI-8`/`RI-11`/`RI-12`/`RI-14` (gated growth, single-authoritative-state)
survive only as a stretched application ("a Registry update is itself a
claim that must survive review before becoming authoritative") — plausible,
but noticeably thinner than the fit at the 3-principle level.

**Net verdict on this compression**: the one-sentence principle is real
and well-evidenced for roughly 10 of 18 Recovered Ideas and both
directly-tested Repeated Themes (`RT-1`, `RT-4`), but it is not a theory
of the whole archive, and presenting it as one would be exactly the
forced coherence this task's Critical Rule prohibits. See
`COUNTER-THEORY.md` and `FINAL-VERDICT.md`.
