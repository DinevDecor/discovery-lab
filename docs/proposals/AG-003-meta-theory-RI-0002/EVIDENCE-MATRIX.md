# Evidence Matrix — RI-0002

Per `META-THEORY-REPORT.md` Q4. Four measures, kept separate rather than
blended into one invented score — this task asked to measure and rank by
evidence, not to manufacture a composite confidence number without a
defensible formula. Ranking is stated qualitatively below the table,
reasoned from the raw measures, not computed by a hidden weighting.

## Definitions used

- **Recurrence** — count of citations AG-002 itself recorded for the
  same underlying claim (its own `RT-N` groupings), not a re-derivation
  by this report.
- **Independent appearance** — whether the recurrence is genuinely
  separate re-derivation (different wording, different date, no stated
  dependency) versus a dependent revision of the same candidate under
  its own testing (`RT-4`'s v1→v4 is dependent evolution, not
  independent re-derivation — a distinction worth keeping, not
  collapsing).
- **Temporal persistence** — days between first and last citation, using
  the dated entries `PILOT-RUN-0002-recovery-report.md` itself cites.
- **Conceptual centrality** — count of other Recovered Ideas this one
  structurally explains or is explained by, per `DEPENDENCY-GRAPH.md`
  (summarized here, computed there).

## Ranking by evidence (not subjective importance)

1. **`RT-3` ("nature as a library of architectures")** — highest raw
   recurrence (5), genuinely independent restatement (not dependent
   revision), longest persistence among repeated *claims* (16 days).
   The single strongest evidentiary signal in the archive, exactly as
   `PILOT-RUN-0002-recovery-report.md` itself already concluded — this
   report does not discover this ranking, it confirms it independently
   from the same numbers.
2. **`RI-15` (Generation–Validation Separation)** — lower raw recurrence
   (1, as a finding) but the **highest conceptual centrality** by a wide
   margin (5 structural connections) and the only finding that cites
   cross-domain convergence evidence *within its own text*. Ranked
   second, not first, because its evidentiary weight is structural and
   source-claimed, not independently re-counted the way `RT-3`'s is —
   see `META-THEORY-REPORT.md` Q6, hypothesis 3, for why this
   distinction matters.
3. **`RT-4` / `RI-1`/`RI-2` / `RI-10`** — a cluster with real, but
   dependent or singular, evidence: `RT-4` is the strongest *applied*
   proof (a real principle actually tested and revised), `RI-1`/`RI-2`
   and `RI-10` are the clearest *stated* versions of the validation
   discipline. Ranked below `RT-3`/`RI-15` because none independently
   recurs the way `RT-3` does, nor centralizes the way `RI-15` does —
   each is strong on one axis only.
4. **Everything else** — single-occurrence, zero-persistence, low- or
   zero-centrality. Real findings, not weak ones on their own terms, but
   the *evidence for including them in a meta-theory* is weaker than the
   above by every measure in this matrix. `RI-18` and `RT-2` rank
   lowest — zero centrality, and in `RT-2`'s case, a connection AG-002
   itself already declined to claim.
