# Deliverable 9 — Final Verdict

## Verdict: **READY FOR OBSERVATION PILOT**

Scoped precisely, not broadly.

## What "ready" means here, exactly

Ready for the **human-invoked pilot** specified in
`6-PILOT-SPECIFICATION.md` — a single run, on `discovery-lab` alone,
producing a Report and (if warranted) a Gate-checked Recommendation for
a real human decision. **Not** a claim that autonomous, self-triggered
observation is ready — `DL-002` already found, and this proposal's own
`3-EVENT-CLASSIFICATION.md` reconfirms per-category, that no trigger
mechanism exists anywhere in this ecosystem for anything to run without
a human first asking for it. This verdict does not contradict `DL-002`;
it answers a narrower, currently-answerable question.

## Why `READY`, not `NOT READY`

Every one of the pilot's own stated requirements (`6-PILOT-
SPECIFICATION.md`) is met today: no new governance needed (reuses
`AG-001`'s `Prototype` discipline and `PROP-0001`'s `ACCEPTED` rules);
no execution runtime needed (human-invoked, like every real task this
session has performed); reversible (read-only); measurable
(`7-SUCCESS-METRICS.md`'s eight metrics, all computable from one run);
objective evidence guaranteed by the schema's own mandatory `Evidence`
field. The design was checked against `PROP-0001`, `Unified Control
Plane`, Human Final Authority, and the Formal Gate concept
(`1-OBSERVATION-AGENT-ARCHITECTURE.md`'s citation table) and introduces
none of the forbidden new mechanisms (Deliverable 4). A real, live
finding (the `AG-001` `STATUS.yaml` mismatch) was produced during this
proposal's own preparation, ahead of the pilot even running — direct,
if incidental, evidence the underlying discipline finds real things.

## Why not a broader `READY`

Declaring readiness for unattended operation would overstate what this
proposal establishes and would contradict `DL-002`'s own, more recent
and more thoroughly checked finding on exactly that question. This
verdict is deliberately narrower than the task's own "autonomous
Observation Agent" framing suggests, for the same reason `EXEC-001`
reported `BLOCKED` rather than assume a favorable Gate result was
sufficient, and `ARCH-003` reported `GO, conditional` rather than an
unconditional yes: precision about what was actually established beats
a cleaner-sounding but overstated verdict.

## What happens next is not this proposal's decision

Whether to authorize the pilot, and separately, whether to later begin
the Draft-stage work of actually writing `AG-004`'s `CONTRACT.md` and
sibling documents, are both human decisions — this proposal recommends,
per Discovery Lab's own Principle 0, and decides neither.
