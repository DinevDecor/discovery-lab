asserted once. Removing either GRIF would weaken confidence in the
other's significance without invalidating it — the `supports` test in
`RELATIONSHIP-ONTOLOGY.md`.

## Why this type, and not a confusable alternative

- **Not `derived_from` or `inspired`**: both require a directional
  origin claim (which one came first, which one prompted the other).
  Both GRIFs appear in the same entry with no stated order of authorship
  or citation of one within the other — directionality is `UNKNOWN`
  from the cited text, so a directional type would overclaim.
- **Not `alternative_to`**: that type fits *competing* candidate
  solutions to the same problem. These two are not competitors — nothing
  in the source suggests choosing one over the other; they read as two
  applications of a compatible discipline to two different projects.
- **Not `depends_on`**: neither GRIF's validity, as stated, is
  conditional on the other remaining true — each is self-contained.
- **Not a merge (`KMP`)**: they are explicitly under different project
  names, and `RI-11` itself only says the Cognitive Sovereignty line
  "appears" related, not that it is the same claim — evidence of
  distinction (different project, separate naming) outweighs evidence of
  identity here, unlike the closer call in
  `KMP-0001-kod-architecture-descriptions.md`.

## Recommendation
